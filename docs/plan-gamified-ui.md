# Gamified UI

The web app reads as software: verb pages, forms, numbers first. This plan moves it toward a game
while every existing feature keeps working. The reference is the clickable game-feel prototype
(Shore hub, Pulse countdown, egg hatch, loot chest, Tower, Hall of Champions, Team 3v3 board).

## Principles

- **Design system first.** Restyle the shared UI kit (`NeonButton`, `NeonCard`, `TabSwitch`) and
  the app shell before touching any feature, so every page changes at once and no feature code
  moves. Props and class names stay stable, so existing tests keep passing.
- **Features stay where they are.** Routes, hooks, controller hooks and chain flows are not
  rewritten. New scenes compose the same `@shared/core` hooks the current pages use.
- **No new runtime dependencies.** Sound is synthesised with the Web Audio API, motion is CSS and
  the Web Animations API, particles are a small canvas.
- **Respect the player.** Sound has a persisted mute switch and only starts after a user gesture.
  `prefers-reduced-motion` stills looping effects.
- **Web only.** Sound, particles and scenes live in `frontend`, not `@shared/core`, because they
  depend on browser APIs that React Native does not have.

## Phases

| Phase | Scope | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundation | Game fonts (Bungee display, Fredoka body) and motion tokens. Chunky 3D `NeonButton`, `TabSwitch`, top bar and sidebar. Sound provider with a mute switch, button taps and toast cues. | `pnpm --filter frontend lint:check`, `test`, `build`, and a visual pass on every route |
| 2. The Shore | `/main` becomes a living scene of the player's own pets with places that open existing routes (Arena, Nest, Hall, Bag, Training). The Pulse orb follows the real drand quicknet clock. The gallery stays on the page. | Same commands, plus wallet-connected and disconnected visual passes |
| 3. Battle feel | Pulse countdown before a fight resolves, damage pops, critical shake, victory and defeat stamps, for duels and Team 3v3, driven by the existing battle controller hooks. | Same commands, plus the battle hook tests |
| 4. Feature scenes | Breeding egg hatch, level-up and training bursts, inventory loot reveal, leaderboard podium. | Same commands |
| 6. Game screens and previews | Every feature screen laid out like the prototype rather than as a form: portraits to pick from instead of dropdowns, combatants on the arena floor, big game keys. Planned features shown as clearly labelled previews (`ComingSoon`): the Tower, daily quests with a streak and chest, season leagues. The prototype's Ledger Stone shows a duel's receipt checks. | Same commands, plus a visual pass per screen |
| 7. Playground and game screens | No page reads as a dashboard. Pets are met on the Shore: tapping one opens its character sheet, and the dock's Pets key opens the party (every pet, and summoning); the `/pets` collection page is gone. Then every feature page is rebuilt as a scene rather than a panel of controls. The architecture may change freely; every feature, chain flow and check stays. | Same commands, plus desktop and 390px visual passes per screen |
| 5. Game frame | The dashboard frame goes: no sidebar, no top bar. Every page is drawn on a full-screen scene (the Shore, the arena, the nest, the Hall of Champions, the market), with a game HUD over the top and a dock over the bottom. The Shore becomes the whole home screen and the gallery moves to `/pets`. This also replaces the old phone layout, where the sidebar took most of a small screen. | Same commands, plus desktop and 390px visual passes |

## Status

- Phase 7, step 2 (game screen frame): done. `DashboardPanel` no longer draws a glass box: a page stands on its scene, its title on a swallow-tailed banner hanging from two ropes (trimmed in the page's accent through `--banner`), with a round back key in the corner. The dashboard-era overrides in `interactions.css` are gone: the left-aligned header with its accent rule, the tinted wash box behind each action page, and the flat translucent restyle of the main action button, which is why primary keys looked faded.
- Phase 7, final sweep: done. Every route was loaded at 390, 768 and 1024 pixels wide and measured for two things: a page that scrolls sideways, and any element past the screen's edge that is not inside something meant to scroll (pet strips) or hidden decoration (the scene backdrops, which overhang on purpose). Nothing scrolled sideways at any width. One element failed: at 768 the Hall of Champions' sign, wider than the Hall, was clipped by the Shore's left edge, so the Hall now stands further in. The Bag, the Hall and Messages could only be swept as their sign-in prompts; their content was checked earlier on temporary pages.
- Phase 7, step 3, the last forms: done. The arena's Defence tab is a guard post: a shield sign says whether the gate is open, closed, or out of date after a rules change; two keys choose all pets or chosen ones, the chosen ones tapped in and out as portraits; the terms sit on a pill and the battle session on its own card. It had no tests, and now has three. The team plan editor keeps its tactical layout, with its sections on pennant tags, its boxes as game cards and its inputs as glass pills. The bag's Equipment tab is a character screen: the pet on its pedestal and its three gear slots on the card beside it, the pet picked from the portrait tray. `PetShowcase` and the placeholder skeleton styles lost their last users and are deleted, with the breed page's dead `.action-controls` rule.
- Phase 7, step 3, arena and dialogs: done. The duel setup puts both combatants on the floor as pedestals (`PetStage`, your fighter under a cyan spotlight, the rival under magenta) with rarity, owner, stat chips and an HP bar beneath, side pennants above, and a VS burst on the win-chance spine between them; an empty side keeps its pedestal with a "?" and says why it is empty. The team challenge shares those pennants and the VS burst, and picks its rival from crest cards (a radio group) instead of a native dropdown, with a resting team shown but not choosable. `NeonModal`, which every dialog uses (summoning, sending, item details, accepting a marriage, the network switchers), is a game dialog: a deep violet card on a glowing ring, the title in the display face, a round close key. The summoning dialog's copy said a wallet could create only one pet, which its own escalating mint fee contradicts; it is now "Summon a Pet" and says each one costs a little more than the last.
- Phase 7, step 3, second pages: done. Marriage proposes at an altar: your pet on a pedestal picked from a portrait tray, the partner's pet (found with the existing search, its art from the chain roster) on the other, and between them a rose arch with a beating heart and the proposal key; the tab keys, married pairs and proposal rows are game cards with round, ringed portraits. The Bag is a stitched leather backpack with a strap and buckle, each category a pouch under a leather name tag, unclaimed drops a gold-rimmed loot row with a gold Claim key, and "Use items on" the same strip of portraits every page picks pets from. The Hall of Champions' lower ranks are champion plaques with the rank on a gold shield, the player's own lit gold, down the middle of the Hall; the search is a pill and the pager round keys. Messages are sealed letters with wax seals, the conversation an open letter case, the composer a writing pad with a violet Send key. The Bag, the Hall and Messages need a signed-in session, so each was checked on a temporary page drawing its real classes with sample data, since deleted.
- Phase 7, step 3 (pages as scenes), first pages: done. Train, Level up and Rename stand on a shared `PetStage` (`components/game/pet-stage`): the chosen pet floats in a spotlight over a lit pedestal, a card beside it shows what the action does (the XP bar, a big Lv 3 to Lv 4 jump, the new name carved on a wooden plaque) with the fee on a coin and the action key, and the roster sits below as a tray. Rename now picks from the same portrait tray instead of a dropdown. The Breeding Lab is a nest: both parents on pedestals with their stat chips and cycle keys, and between them a straw nest with the egg, its generation, the name field and the breed key; the spouse tab puts your pet and its partner either side of the same nest. The lab's invented compatibility percentage and its guessed rarity are gone: the chain decides rarity from randomness when the egg settles, and the nest now says so.
- Phase 7, step 1 (pets on the Shore): done. Tapping a pet on the Shore opens its sheet (`components/party/pet-sheet`): art, rarity, level, skill, HP, XP, record, DNA stats, gear and cooldowns, from the same reads the collection card used, with keys for Battle, Train, Level up, Breed, Rename and Gear, plus Send and the Story and Talk previews. The dock's Pets key opens the party (`components/party/party-drawer`): every pet highest level first, since the beach draws eight, and a Summon tile. Both live in `PartyProvider` (`contexts/party`) inside the game frame, so any page can open them. The sheet's keys open each page with the pet chosen, through `usePetChoice`, which only applies the handed-over pet once it is among the panel's eligible pets, so a pet on cooldown arrives unchosen instead of chosen and bound to fail. `/pets` now redirects home; the gallery, its controller hook, its rank strip and the My Pets tile are deleted. An empty beach invites the first summon.
- Phase 1: done on `feat/gamified-ui`. Every route checked visually at desktop width.
- Phase 2: done. The Shore sits above the gallery on `/main`. Its size follows the scene's own width through a container query, because a pinned sidebar makes the scene far narrower than the window. The client is never told which drand round a battle committed to, so the Pulse moment shows the live beat while the battle is `accepted` or `committed`, and never claims a round number. The cards were checked visually through the battle overlay preview; the podium was not, since the leaderboard needs a signed-in session.

- Phase 6 history: Done: `PetPicker` is a roster of rarity-ringed portraits (training, level-up, rename, equipment and the team grids use it); the duel setup puts both combatants on the arena floor with portrait rosters below; the Tower preview at `/tower` (a place on the Shore and a Soon tile in More); the daily quests preview in the HUD; the season leagues preview in the Hall of Champions. The bag's tiles, messages and the pet collection's cards followed, as the status line above says.
- Ledger Stone: done. A duel's result screen has a Ledger Stone key next to Watch Again and Leave. It opens the prototype's carved tablet, where each check the browser already ran on the signed receipt (`useVerifiedBattleReceipt`) lights as a rune: the operator's signature, the drand beacon, the seed, the replay and the XP. A failed check shows the verifier's own reason. The stone computes nothing; it shows checks that ran before the fight was animated. The link to the previous receipt is not a rune, because one receipt has nothing to check it against, so the stone says the public verifier checks it across the whole ledger. Team battles have no stone yet: their result hook checks the seed and the combat log, not the full set, so a stone there would claim more than was checked. The result keys became chunky game keys in the same change, for duels and Team 3v3. Checked visually on the battle overlay preview at desktop width and at 390px.
- AI companions preview: done. A pet's card on the Shore has a Talk key beside Battle. It opens a companion panel about that pet previewing roadmap §10 in a `ComingSoon` frame: sample traits, the pet proposing a training session that waits on the player's signature, and journal entries each citing a Ledger Stone. Its note states the roadmap's rules in the player's words: a pet only proposes, the wallet signs every action, and personality never changes a fight. The wider card also showed that a pet near either end of a phone's beach had its card cut off by the scene's edge; such a card now opens inward.
- No invented figures: done. The pet collection's strip showed a made-up leaderboard and a fixed "#3 Global rank" (placeholders from the first gallery redesign), and the HUD's streak chip a made-up "7 day streak". The strip now reads the real player board and the player's real rank, the same reads the HUD and the Hall of Champions use, and the streak chip says streaks are coming. Both rank reads run only for a signed-in session, and the HUD used to read "no answer yet" as unranked; a signed-out player is now asked to sign in instead.
- Pet stories preview: done. Each pet card has an amber Story key between Battle and Send. It opens a storybook about that pet (its real name, art, generation and level) previewing the planned stories (roadmap §8) inside a `ComingSoon` frame: five chapters, one per arc the roadmap names (origin, lineage, a level milestone, a rival, marriage) with what would unlock each, and a sample first chapter ending in a choice. Nothing is generated, stored or unlocked. Its open state lives in `usePetGallery`, like the Send dialog's.
- Night Market preview: done. `/market` previews the planned marketplace (roadmap §7) inside a `ComingSoon` frame, with a Soon tile in More: sample stalls of pets and real catalog items, each framed in its rarity colour and priced in the active chain's coin, plus the player's empty stall and the three steps of a trade. It reads and writes nothing.
- Ledger Stones page: done. `/ledger` (a place on the Shore between the training ground and the market, and a tile in More) shows the player's newest 24 battles as stones, duels and Team 3v3 alike, from the public receipt corpus (`/api/receipts/by-wallet`, which gained `order=desc` for this). Opening one fetches its receipt again and runs the same five checks as the duel result, through one shared `fetchAndCheckReceipt`, so the two screens cannot disagree; the list's own summary is never what a rune reports. A real team receipt was read this way and every check held. The panel's back key now takes its own row on phones, where it used to sit over a two-line caption on every page. Checked visually at desktop width and at 390px.
- Title screen: done. The first visit home in a browser session opens on the prototype's title: the logo over a night sky, The Quiet Shore, and Tap to enter. The tap, or Enter, fades it onto the Shore with the level-up jingle, which is also the gesture browsers require before sound can play. A link straight to any other page skips it, and so does every later visit home in the session (`sessionStorage`). Checked visually at desktop width and at 390px.
- Phase 5: done. The sidebar, top bar and pin hook are deleted; their behaviour moved rather than vanished. The HUD shows the player's real rank and wins (the old rank footer), the placeholder daily quests behind a scroll key, the sound switch and the wallet. The dock has keys for Pets, Nest, Battle, Bag and Ranks, and a More sheet lists every page, with team battles only while the backend accepts them. Page panels are translucent so their scene shows through, and the arena has the prototype's Duel or Team 3v3 switch. On a phone the HUD puts the wallet on a second row.
